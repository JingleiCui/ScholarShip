# ScholarShip 顶部导航与纵向内容流

按用户反馈重做：取消左右侧栏，只使用顶部导航；统一蓝白主视觉，借鉴内容平台的横向分区和纵向浏览。7 个页面均为 GPT Image 概念图，示例内容不作为科研证据；本轮不修改前端代码。

## 01-home · 首页

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Generate one polished high-fidelity DESKTOP website UI screenshot for ScholarShip 学者之船, Chinese academic research assistant. Entirely NEW design direction: contemporary content platform, inspired by the browsing rhythm of Bilibili, NOT a management console. Wide landscape 16:10 composition, front facing, no monitor frame or browser chrome. STRICT: NO left sidebar, NO right sidebar, NO vertical navigation, NO split admin shell, NO right assistant panel, NO dark mode. Navigation is ONLY a slim horizontal TOP BAR spanning full width: small cobalt origami sail + ScholarShip wordmark, seven concise tabs '首页', '文献库', '阅读', '发现', '图谱', '写作', '绘图', a rounded global search field and small round avatar. One centered flowing page, max width approx 1280px, white outer margins. Page clearly continues vertically BELOW the viewport: show the start of another content section cut by bottom edge and small unobtrusive scrollbar on far right. No side rail of any kind. Horizontal pill filters directly below top navigation where relevant. Unified blue-white design system: pure white #FFFFFF, airy cool white #F6F9FF, navy readable ink #19345C, primary clear sky/cobalt #3978F6, pale blue #EAF2FF, soft neutral divider #E4EBF5. Very subtle pastel-blue highlights, never large decorative gradients. Friendly modern Chinese sans-serif, clean 14-16px body, 26-32px main title, crisp icons, 12px media-card corners, subtle borders, no heavy shadows. Restrained soft playfulness appropriate for students, content-first like a modern knowledge discovery platform. Use intentional hierarchy and ample whitespace. Not dashboard KPIs, no analytics tiles or admin tables, no developer/API settings. Add small '设计预览' near project name; all displayed content is demo illustration. Beautiful credible Chinese text, concise labels, practical visible interactions. Screens should feel designed as ONE coherent website with exactly the same top nav and palette, rather than separate themes. No giant marketing hero or large slogan; show useful content.

SCREEN SPECIFIC:
ACTIVE TOP TAB 首页. A lively but calm research home content feed. Below horizontal nav: greeting '今天，继续探索。' with compact project dropdown 'Transformer 研究' aligned inline, not sidebar. A horizontal elegant search/question box '搜索论文，或向文献提问' with a blue '开始探索' button. One horizontal row of interest chips '为你推荐', '自然语言处理', '计算机视觉', '我的收藏'. Main feature is a four-column media-card grid with attractive scholarly cover thumbnails: miniature paper pages plus restrained diagrams or small attention matrices, never generic scenic photos. Cards titled 'Attention Is All You Need', 'Layer Normalization', 'BERT：双向语言表示', '长序列建模阅读笔记', with authors/year and tiny '论文' / '阅读笔记' label, no fake viewer statistics. Like a premium research content homepage, not an office dashboard. Below, a full-width '继续阅读' horizontal strip containing 3 compact reading items and progress marks, then beginning of another four-card row titled '你的研究动态' clipped below fold. No left/right side panels. White background and blue controls.
```
